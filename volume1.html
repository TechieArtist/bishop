THE BREAKDOWN

The distortion, in brief

1800s

First distortion begins in Kowloon.

1960s

Breaking begins widespread purging.

2000s

Media coverage of Tanpeki begins.

The distortions began on the Kowloon peninsula. Dividing its irregular population by 0.3, a factor drawn from the Milky Way's initial mass function (IMF), gives an approximation of 43.00, a number whose meaning is not yet understood, but is consequential. Some speculate that it is related to the number of distortions yet to occur.
