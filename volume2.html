Short Story — 読み切り

Baby, close your eyes

ベイビー、目を閉じて

Rinne is a nurse who sees the vitality of the people around her as a coloured halo above their heads. The story follows her through one hospital shift, one night at home, and one night back in the ER.

りんねは看護師で、まわりの人の生命力が、頭の上に色のハローとして見える。物語は、病院での勤務、家での一夜、そしてERに戻る一夜をたどる。
