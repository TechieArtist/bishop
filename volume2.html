“Perception is the prison.”

Notes headed 丹碧, August 2023
Abstract

Tanpeki is a perceptual phenomenon in which vitality appears as a colored halo above a person's head — pink for Deteriorating, cyan for Stable, green for Healthy. Only certain females can perceive the halos; to males, they are only distortion, and life stays invisible. “Baby, close your eyes” follows Rinne, a nurse who can see them.

01 · Cone adaptation

The plots track what the fovea is adapted to against what neutral gray looks like now, as the cones fatigue. The classical account of chromatic adaptation is von Kries [2].

02 · The opponent basis

The plot axes are the cardinal directions of color space [6], as measured in the lateral geniculate nucleus [7] — built on the MacLeod–Boynton chromaticity diagram [5]. The opponent account begins with Hering [1]; its quantitative form is Hurvich and Jameson [4].

03 · Afterimage

The 2023 notes record a bright yellow field shifting toward cyan and magenta as the receptors fatigue.
