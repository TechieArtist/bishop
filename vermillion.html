Vermillion Zyu Hung (朱紅)

Kowloon Breaking Tanpeki Vermillion

From VISION Magazine, March 1, 1967.

Vermillion Zyu Hung is a fashion and beauty house founded in the 1920s, producing tailored skirts, jewelry, and makeup for women. Relaunched in recent years by French designer Gabriel François and Hong Kong expat Angel Wing-sze, the brand has been reimagined for a new generation while keeping its heritage craftsmanship.

The two collaborators bonded over a shared commitment to raising awareness of domestic abuse faced by women across Europe and Asia. A portion of the brand's proceeds is donated to organizations that support survivors.

The brand has recently come under scrutiny after reports that some of its cotton and mica pigments are sourced from suppliers that also hold contracts with U.S. defense contractors, including companies linked to nuclear weapons programs. Activists have questioned whether a brand built on ethical values is quietly profiting from the arms economy, and have called for greater transparency in its supply chain.
