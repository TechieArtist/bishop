Kowloon Breaking Tanpeki

The distortions began on the Kowloon peninsula. Dividing its irregular population by 0.3, a factor drawn from the Milky Way's initial mass function (IMF), gives an approximation of 43.00, a number whose meaning is not yet understood, but is consequential.

The first distortion

The first distortion begins in Kowloon, in the 1800s. People of Kowloon is the work set there.
