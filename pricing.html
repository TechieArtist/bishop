Introduction

The New Era

When I created The Atelier/Propitiation, I wanted to establish a simple idea: digital art is more than art made with computers—it has its own distinct spirit. Through the loss of tactile presence that's of drawing, this physical experience has been altered into a mental experience where thoughts give way to shape.

CHAPTER III

Hell

The Atelier/Propitiation presents a form of abstraction peculiar to this moment in art history. By using artificial intelligence frame by frame rather than through conventional generation, each frame becomes an independent possibility. The weight of the past no longer determines the future. Like the Italian Futurists' attempts to depict movement through successive moments, each second exists as its own potential image. Was art made to create artificial intelligence, or was artificial intelligence made to create art?
