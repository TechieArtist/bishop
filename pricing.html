Introduction

The New Era

When I sought out to create The Atelier exhibition I wanted establish one key thing, that digital art was something beyond the artwork made from computers, that theirs a distinct spirit to it. Through the lost of tactile feel that’s natural to drawing digital art gives a more abstract feeling to what drawing could be. It’s as if this physical experience has been altered into a mental experience where thoughts give way to shape.
