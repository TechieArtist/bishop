SYSTEMS

Tanpeki (丹碧)

Kowloon Breaking Tanpeki

Tanpeki (丹碧) is a perceptual phenomenon that distorts how color is seen. The term was coined by scientists in Hokkaido, who took the 丹 from 丹田, the body's energy center. Only certain females of a species can read a spectrum of vitality through the distortion, seeing how alive a thing is as shifts in color. Males are affected by Tanpeki too, but to them it is only distortion, and life stays invisible.

The halo

A perceptual phenomenon in which vitality appears as a colored halo above a person's head. Only certain females can perceive these halos.

  • Deteriorating — pink
  • Stable — cyan
  • Healthy — green

The school

照顔学校 (Shiyōugan) is an all-girls school in Tokyo for women who can perceive life through Tanpeki.

In V

Tanpeki is Volume 2 — Baby, close your eyes, following Rinne, a nurse who sees the vitality of the people around her as a coloured halo above their heads.
