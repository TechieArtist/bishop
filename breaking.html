Breaking (割れる)

Distortion Kowloon Breaking Tanpeki Vermillion

Breaking is a malware that, once spread throughout your hardware, scrapes your entire storage device before purging it.

It is also speculated that the malware was the genesis of cloud storage, merging the entire internet's storage into one.

Rumors claim it was developed by the Chinese government during the 1960s computer arms race. It is said to have lain dormant for decades, resurfacing in the 1990s as the web took off, when it began targeting pornographic sites. Its effectiveness has endured into the modern era, as physical storage grew increasingly obsolete, but the rumor remains unconfirmed.

In V

Breaking is the 1960s entry in the Events timeline in Volume 1 — Prologue.
