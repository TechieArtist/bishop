Whether something counts as a distortion is decided by the government's classification rule. A distortion requires at least two of three conditions:

  1. A documented deviation from the established laws of nature.
  2. Capable of global expansion, not limited to one location.
  3. The possibility of destabilizing society if left unchecked.
